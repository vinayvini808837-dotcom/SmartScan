Statutory Framework

Ministry of Consumer Affairs & Legal Metrology

Ensuring fair trade practices, mandatory consumer transparency, and standard measurements across all 28 States and 8 Union Territories of India.

⚖️

The Legal Metrology Act, 2009

Act No. 1 of 2010 mandates that every pre-packaged commodity sold in Indian retail or e-commerce must declare standard weight, clear MRP, and manufacturer origin to protect buyers from fraud.

🛡️

Packaged Commodities Rules (PCR), 2011

Enacted under Section 52(2), Rule 6 defines 7 non-negotiable declarations including unit sale price, date of manufacture, and a functional consumer care cell with phone and email.

🏛️

State Enforcement Officers

Each state has an authorized Controller and Assistant Controllers of Legal Metrology empowered to seize non-compliant goods, issue notices, and levy compounding penalties under Section 36.
